Liquid Budget → ProjectionLab Sync

Connect both apps, match accounts once, then sync balances whenever you like.

1Connect

Create one under Liquid Budget → Developer → API Tokens. A read-only token (lbro_) is all this needs.
In ProjectionLab, open Account Settings → Plugins, turn on Enable plugins, and copy the key. Keep a signed-in ProjectionLab tab open while testing and syncing.

2Match accounts

Pick the ProjectionLab account each Liquid Budget account should update. Archived Liquid Budget accounts are left out. Matches save as you go.

3Preview & sync

Preview what will change in ProjectionLab, then sync. Credit cards, loans and mortgages are sent to ProjectionLab debts or asset loans as a positive amount owed.
